Portable Ventilator V7 - Maintenance Manual (Synthetic Excerpt)

Section 1: Scheduled Service
The V7 ventilator requires full service every 5 months, including flow
sensor inspection, filter replacement, and battery health check. Flow
sensors degrade faster in high-humidity wards and should be checked
more frequently in those locations.

Section 2: Emergency Procedures
If the ventilator reports a "low pressure" fault during active use,
switch the patient to the backup unit immediately and tag the faulty
unit as out_of_service. Do not attempt in-field repair while a unit is
assigned to a patient.

Section 3: Battery Guidance
Battery packs should be replaced after 300 charge cycles or 18 months,
whichever comes first, regardless of apparent battery health.
